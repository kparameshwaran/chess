FIDE Chess Standards & Controls

FIDE Tournament Standard Rules

  • Legal Moves Only: Pieces can only make FIDE-legal moves. Moves that expose or fail to defend your King from check are forbidden.
  • Castling (O-O / O-O-O): Allowed if neither King nor chosen Rook has moved, no pieces intervene, and the King does not castle out of, through, or into check.
  • En Passant: If an opposing pawn advances two squares on its initial move and lands adjacent to your pawn, you may capture it diagonally on the very next turn.
  • Pawn Promotion: Reaching the 8th rank immediately opens the promotion selector (Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight).
  • Draw Conditions: Stalemate, 50-move rule without pawn push or capture, 3-fold position repetition, or insufficient mating material.
